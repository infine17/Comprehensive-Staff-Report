Department Structure
3 suggestions
1
Dedicated Ticket Department
🔥 P1

Assign 2–3 Full/Head Mods specifically to handle tickets full-time with a [Ticket Dept] role tag. No new hiring needed — just formalise existing staff.

Why it works
  • Tickets currently fall on whoever's online — inconsistent response times
  • Ticket ownership = faster resolutions + clear accountability
  • Directly enforces existing policy (Trial Mods can't solve tickets)
  • Zero cost — uses existing staff, zero new hiring
⏱ 10 min setup 🟢 Very High Impact No budget needed
2
Media / Content Moderation Role
⚡ P2

Assign 1–2 mods specifically to monitor media channels, image posts, and file uploads for spam, NSFW, and malware links.

Why it works
  • Most servers get hit with NSFW/spam in media channels before chat mods notice
  • Specialized eyes = faster catches = less damage to server reputation
  • Easy to implement — assign existing reliable mods to a new role tag
⏱ 5 min setup 🟢 High Impact
3
Trial Mod Mentorship Pairing
⚡ P2

Each Trial Mod gets officially paired with a Full Mod as their mentor. The mentor guides and vouches for them during trial period.

Why it works
  • Reduces leadership burden — mods train mods, not heads train mods
  • Gives Trial Mods clear guidance on what's expected
  • Makes promotions easier — mentor can formally vouch
  • Zero budget, zero new systems, zero new tools needed

Automation & Bots
3 suggestions
4
Auto-Warning System (MEE6 / Wick Bot)
🔥 P1

Configure automated escalation: warn → mute → kick → ban for common violations like spam, slurs, raid-pings, and excessive caps.

Why it works
  • Mods don't have to manually type warnings for obvious violations
  • Consistent punishment across all mods — eliminates bias claims
  • MEE6/Wick used in 90%+ of large Discord servers — proven solution
  • Takes 15 minutes to configure — zero ongoing maintenance
⏱ 15 min setup 🟢 Very High Impact Bot: MEE6 or Wick
5
Verification Gate
⚡ P2

New members must complete a simple verification (react to message, answer question, or solve a captcha) before accessing the server.

Why it works
  • Stops bot raids and nuker accounts completely
  • Reduces Trial Mod workload on dealing with obvious bot accounts
  • Built into Carl-bot / Wick Bot — zero coding, zero cost
  • Best practice for any Discord server above 100 members
⏱ 15 min setup 🟢 High Impact Bot: Carl-bot / Wick
6
StatBot Weekly Auto-Report (Already Installed)
📌 P3

Use the existing StatBot to auto-generate a weekly staff activity report in a private leadership channel. Zero new bots needed.

Why it works
  • StatBot is already installed and tracking all 17 staff members
  • Leadership can check staff activity passively — no manual auditing
  • Takes 5 minutes to configure the scheduled report command
  • Saves hours of manual tracking per month
⏱ 5 min setup 🟡 Medium Impact Already installed

Process & Policy
3 suggestions
7
Formal LOA (Leave of Absence) System
🔥 P1

Create a #loa-requests channel with a pinned format template. Staff officially announce planned inactivity in advance.

Why it works
  • Right now inactive staff just go ghost — leadership has zero warning
  • LOA system = mods stay accountable without being punished for real life
  • Reduces unnecessary demotions of good mods with temporary issues
  • Zero setup cost — just create a channel + pinned message template

⏱ 5 min setup 🟢 Very High Impact Zero cost
8
Monthly Activity Minimum Standards
⚡ P2

Write and publish clear, measurable activity minimums for each staff rank — visible to all staff in a pinned document.

RoleMin. Messages/WeekMin. Mod Actions/Week
Trial Mod30 messages3 warns/mutes
Full Mod50 messages8 warns/mutes
Head Mod30 messagesLeadership duties count
Why it works
  • Currently no written standard — demotions feel subjective and cause drama
  • With written standards, demotions/promotions are obvious and drama-free
  • StatBot already tracks all this data — just need to write the policy
⏱ 30 min (write doc) 🟡 High Impact
9
Standardised Strike System for Staff
📌 P3

An internal 3-strike system for staff discipline. Transparent, consistent, and removes bias from leadership decisions.

Strike Triggers (example)
  • Strike 1: Unexplained inactivity over 1 week
  • Strike 2: Unprofessional behaviour in public channels
  • Strike 3: Policy violation (e.g., Trial Mod handling a ticket without assignment) → demotion
⏱ ~1 hr (write policy) 🟡 Medium-High Impact

Community Growth
2 suggestions
10
Member of the Week Recognition
💡 P4

Highlight an active, positive community member each week in announcements. Simple weekly post by any Head Mod.

Why it works
  • Encourages positive engagement — members compete for recognition
  • Costs nothing, takes 5 minutes per week
  • Carl-bot starboard/highlight feature already handles nominations
⏱ 5 min/week 🟢 Easy win
11
Server Feedback / Suggestions Channel
💡 P4

A #suggestions channel with upvote/downvote reactions. Carl-bot auto-formats posts and threshold-based pins highlight the best ideas.

Why it works
  • Members feel heard = better retention and community loyalty
  • Leadership gets real community insights passively without asking
  • Carl-bot suggestion feature is zero-cost and zero-maintenance
⏱ 5 min setup 🟢 Easy win
